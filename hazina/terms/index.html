3. Accuracy

Hazina reads texts and statements written by banks and mobile-money services, whose wording can change. It may sometimes misread one, miss one, or put a payment in the wrong place. Please check the records that matter to you against your bank or M-Pesa statements. Your bank's and mobile-money provider's own records are the official ones.

4. Your data and backups
